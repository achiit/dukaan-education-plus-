Our story

A neighbourhood centre, built the way we wished tuition was.

My younger cousin used to come home from school every day with the same doubts and nobody patient enough to sit with him. I watched him lose confidence in maths long before he lost interest in it. That stayed with me for years.

In 2026 we opened Education Plus in Rewa so that no child in our area has to sit in the back row and stay quiet. We are a new centre, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What we promise is simple: teachers who explain until it makes sense, and parents who always know how their child is doing.

Come for a free demo class. If you like what you see, we would be happy to have you. If not, you have lost nothing.

  • One centre from Nursery to 12th
  • Morning and evening batches to fit school timings
  • Three direct phone numbers, so you always get an answer
